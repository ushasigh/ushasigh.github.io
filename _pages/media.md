## Standardization and Industry Recognition

<ul class="media-list">
  <li class="media-item">
    <a class="media-title" href="https://books.google.com/books?id=rAREEQAAQBAJ&amp;q=EdgeRIC" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Fundamentals of O-RAN</a>
    <span class="media-meta">N. D. Tripathi and V. K. Shah, Wiley-IEEE Press &middot; 2025 &middot; Chapter 3, pp. 115-122</span>
    <span class="media-note">Presents <strong>EdgeRIC</strong> as the real-time RIC (RT RIC) platform at the RAN edge, highlighting its sub-millisecond operation for <strong>TTI-level synchronized</strong> control policies, its RL-based scheduling &mu;Apps, and <strong>SPARC</strong> as a multi-site spectrum management use case built on it.</span>
  </li>
  <li class="media-item">
    <a class="media-title" href="https://mediastorage.o-ran.org/ngrg-rr/nGRG-RR-2024-10-dApp%20use%20cases%20and%20requirements.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">dApps for Real-Time RAN Control: Use Cases and Requirements</a>
    <span class="media-meta">O-RAN Alliance next Generation Research Group (nGRG), Research Report RR-2024-10 &middot; October 2024</span>
    <span class="media-note">Cites <strong>EdgeRIC</strong> as the reference for the real-time RAN Intelligent Controller (RT RIC), a controller dedicated to real-time applications alongside the Near-RT RIC, and compares it with the dApp approach to real-time RAN control.</span>
  </li>
  <li class="media-item">
    <span class="media-title">EdgeRIC on jbpf-integrated OCUDU 5G</span>
    <span class="media-meta">OCUDU Workshop, Washington, DC &middot; October 2026</span>
    <span class="media-note">Live demo of EdgeRIC running on the OCUDU open-source 5G stack with Microsoft's jbpf telemetry framework.</span>
  </li>
</ul>
