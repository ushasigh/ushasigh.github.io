abstract: 'Tiny-Twin is an open-source, full-stack 5G digital twin that runs on commodity CPUs, with no specialized hardware. It combines a complete 5G protocol stack with time-varying multi-tap channel convolution, per-UE channel isolation, channel trace replay, and built-in real-time RIC integration. It scales to multiple UEs while preserving protocol timing, offering a middle ground between low-fidelity simulators and costly hardware emulators.'
