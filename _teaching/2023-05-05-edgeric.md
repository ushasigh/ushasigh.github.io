It is decoupled from the RAN stack but operates at RAN timescales (sub-millisecond), running AI-in-the-loop μApps that use RAN and application-level information to make decisions. EdgeRIC outperforms cloud-based near-real-time RICs, and its AI policies beat model-based strategies by 5% to 25% in throughput and application-level metrics.'
