abstract: 'Robots stream LiDAR, camera, and inertial data over the cellular uplink to an edge application that closes their control loop, and every stream for a timestep must arrive in time for the next action. Uneven arrival across sensors and users adds significant latency. mmsRAN explores coflow-inspired uplink scheduling that delivers each timestep''s sensor streams together, within and across robots.'
